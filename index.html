Physics-Informed Machine Learning and Conformal Prediction for Friction Stir Weld Tensile Strength: A Cross-Source Benchmark
Rohit Kumar, Subhrajyoti Saroj, Priyabrata Sahoo
Models that look strong under random k-fold CV on pooled literature data fall apart when tested on an unseen source. We show why, and pair physics-informed ML with conformal prediction ranges.
